## Privacy requests

You can ask us to:

- Tell you what personal information we have about you and how it's used
- Correct information that's wrong
- Delete your DRVN account and the personal information associated with it

**To delete your account,** use the account deletion option in the DRVN app, or email us.

**For any other request,** email [info@drvnapp.com](mailto:info@drvnapp.com) from the address on your DRVN account, with "Privacy request" in the subject line. Tell us what you're asking for, and, if it's about a teen's account, which account it is. We may need to verify your identity before we complete a request.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to DRVN, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for details on what we collect and how we use it.
